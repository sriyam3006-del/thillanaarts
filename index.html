Thillana Arts

A Hyderabad dance school preserving Bharatanatyam while shaping future artists and teachers.

Founded in 2008 in Hyderabad, Thillana Arts is dedicated to nurturing enthusiastic young dancers through thoughtful Bharatanatyam training. Under Madhavi Marellapudi's guidance, students build technique, confidence, discipline, and a deeper understanding of the classical tradition.

The institute has earned recognition for preparing students for examinations, with many completing Certificate and Diploma courses in Bharatanatyam from Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University. Students also participate in examinations conducted by Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal and have consistently performed with distinction.

About Madhavi

A performer, teacher, and mentor with deep roots in Indian classical dance.

Madhavi Marellapudi holds a postgraduate degree in Performing Arts from the University of Hyderabad and a Diploma in Bharatanatyam from the Andhra Pradesh Education Department. She is a graded artiste of Doordarshan, Hyderabad.

She trained under Sri Avasarala Rukmaji Rao, Sri Pasumarthy Ramalinga Sastry, and Kalaimamani Smt. Roja Kannan. Her artistic journey spans Bharatanatyam performance, teaching, choreography, and a strong familiarity with Kuchipudi Natyam.
